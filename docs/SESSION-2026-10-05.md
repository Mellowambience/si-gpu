# SI-GPU session recap — October 5, 2026

Software-only graphics reconstruction research for an RTX 2080 Ti (11 GiB), i9-9900K and 64 GiB RAM. Primary output is 2560×1440; secondary output is 1920×1080.

## Completed
- Refined the original SI-WARE into SI-GPU 0.4.0 with native D3D12/HLSL spatial reconstruction and temporal fusion.
- Added bounded automated learning, replay data, frozen evaluation, regression gates, model checksums, promotion and rollback. No persistent training service is running.
- Passed 38 automated tests. Both temporal shader variants passed 216-frame parity suites plus edge-case checks; maximum full-suite RGB difference was approximately 6.26e-7 against a 5e-6 tolerance.
- Accepted candidate cycle-000003-candidate-03: approximately +0.065 dB audit improvement over the initial learned model. Stronger still-image candidates were rejected when temporal quality regressed.
- After the game closed, three quiet-GPU runtime checks passed. Combined spatial and temporal steady-history GPU p95 was 1.08–1.24 ms at 1440p and 0.73–0.84 ms at 1080p. Earlier busy-GPU failures remain recorded.

## Limits
These are synthetic-data, offline GPU microbenchmarks. They exclude engine integration, input upload/readback and end-to-end gameplay costs. They do not demonstrate equivalence to DLSS 5, neural rendering, quantum computing or general superintelligence.

## Next session
Integrate the reconstruction pipeline into a controlled rendering application, including motion vectors, camera cuts, jitter, exposure and resource synchronization. Measure complete frame time, image stability and latency at both monitor sizes. Expand evaluation to representative captured scenes before claiming competitive image quality.
